Learn Oktavia

Three paths for partners and customer teams: one to present Oktavia from presales, and two technical ones to install, operate, configure in depth and automate it. Every course includes labs on a real network and an assessment. The courses are currently available in Spanish.

Presales · Associate level

For whoever presents Oktavia to a customer: what the platform is, the concepts to master, how to qualify an opportunity, how to handle objections and how to run the demo. Eight modules, six labs and an interactive exam. Open to everyone.

Technical · Associate level: install, bring up and operate

For the engineer who installs Oktavia in a customer's infrastructure and runs it day to day. Nine modules and seven labs:

  • Platform architecture and container-based installation.
  • Bring-up: organizations, users, sites, inventory, credentials and the first audit.
  • Daily operation, job scheduling and reading compliance.
  • Closing the loop with Argos and from the digital twin.
  • Argos for the operator.
  • Maintenance, backup, upgrades and platform troubleshooting.

For Oktanet partners, with access to a remote lab.

Technical · Professional level: configure in depth, automate and integrate

For the engineer who designs the intent of a whole network, adapts it and automates it. Requires the Associate level.

  • Declared intent in depth: scopes, precedence, service catalog and per-vendor templates.
  • Configuration and state compliance rules.
  • The digital twin per vendor: Cisco, FortiGate, FortiSwitch, Junos and Meraki.
  • Adapting the network architecture with and without Argos.
  • Automating the Configurator intent.
  • Network security, telemetry and event automation.
  • Corporate identity and access control.
  • AI in Oktavia: providers, local mode, cost and correlation.
  • Network troubleshooting with Oktavia and with AI.
  • Automating and orchestrating Oktavia with its API, Python and Ansible.
  • Integration with sources of truth, monitoring, ticketing and security tools.

For Oktanet partners, with access to a remote lab.
