1. What it solves, and who it is for

Enterprise networks grow in complexity faster than the teams that run them. The pattern repeats: manual audits that take days, configuration drift nobody notices until there is an incident, visibility split across four tools, and no traceability over who changed what and with whose authorisation.

The underlying problem is that diagnosis does not close the loop. Audit tools find the finding, but fixing it remains manual work outside the tool, device by device.

Oktavia closes that loop. It discovers the network, audits it against the declared standard, proposes the change, applies it only with approval, and audits again to confirm the finding is closed. Every action leaves a downloadable artefact and stays in a searchable history.

Who it is for: IT and network operations teams running multi-vendor infrastructure that need to move quickly without giving up control — integrators, organisations with multiple sites, and teams answering to internal or regulatory audits.

3. Licensing and commercial model

Two licenses. The complete platform is Oktavia Pro. Continuous telemetry and event orchestration are licensed separately because they have a different consumption profile — they store time series and run a permanent evaluator — and not every network needs them on day one.
